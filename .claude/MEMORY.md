## Open Gate: AI Agent Posture (ADR-018, Proposed)

The corpus decided AI five times as bounded features (ADR-010 translation, ADR-013
activism messaging, ADR-014 concierge, ADR-016 §38 BYOM, `system.md` AI Integration)
and never asked what changes if agents operate throughout the product.
`spec/security.md` and ADR-002 contain zero AI references; `spec/compliance.md`
contains one.

`decisions/018-ai-agent-posture.md` is the project's first `Proposed` ADR. It gates
production — not prototyping — on any capability with cross-feature read scope, tool
authority, autonomous action, or broad credentials. The five accepted features are
unaffected. Diary entry 12 tells the story; ADR-018 lists the eight things the review
must resolve.

Three inconsistencies were recorded in ADR-018. The first (ADR-013 without a
supersession banner) was resolved by ADR-019 with a clarifying banner: ADR-013 never
made a model decision, so nothing in it is superseded. Two remain deliberately
unfixed: `dashboards.md` still calls reallocation suggestions AI-generated after
ADR-016 §49 made them rule-based, and the BYOM settings screen ADR-016 committed to
was never drawn.

## ADR-019: Central Services & Metered Billing (2026-09-28, Accepted)

Every capability is an **internal module** (runs in the tenant instance, flat
subscription) or a **central service** (platform-hosted, executed per tenant under the
tenant's keys and contracts, retains nothing between calls, metered). Third-party
variable costs pass through **at cost with no margin**; GreenGrass earns from the
subscription alone. Catalogue, entitlements and metering are platform-layer metadata.
An affiliation carries `billing_mode` (`member_pays` | `alliance_pays`), set at
onboarding, changed only by both OAs; paying is not seeing. Free-tier metered
allowance is **deferred**. Entitlements are the credential boundary any future agent
inherits — ADR-018 is fed, not resolved. Amends ADR-007 (banner added); clarifies
ADR-013 (banner added). Diary entry 13.

Planned follow-ups from the same Whimsical board: suppression as first catalogue entry
(mvp/workflows/system), the four central services into the comms-intelligence
iterations, content ops from the creator wireframes into press.md, then ADR-018 fed
with the board as the proposal under review.
