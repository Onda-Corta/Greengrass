## Open Gate: AI Agent Posture (ADR-018, Proposed)

The corpus decided AI five times as bounded features (ADR-010 translation, ADR-013
activism messaging, ADR-014 concierge, ADR-016 §38 BYOM, `system.md` AI Integration)
and never asked what changes if agents operate throughout the product.
`spec/security.md` and ADR-002 contain zero AI references; `spec/compliance.md`
contains one.

`decisions/018-ai-agent-posture.md` is the project's first `Proposed` ADR. It gates
production — not prototyping — on any capability with cross-feature read scope, tool
authority, autonomous action, or broad credentials. The five accepted features are
unaffected. Diary entry 12 tells the story; ADR-018 lists the eight things the review
must resolve.

Three inconsistencies are recorded in ADR-018 and deliberately **not** fixed:
ADR-013 is `Accepted` without a supersession banner, `dashboards.md` still calls
reallocation suggestions AI-generated after ADR-016 §49 made them rule-based, and the
BYOM settings screen ADR-016 committed to was never drawn.
