Values the spec leaves open (our choices)

  • Chart palette --color-chart-1…8: Okabe–Ito categorical (colourblind-safe), slate for the eighth.
  • Dark-mode primary, status and subtle colours (the spec says “auto-lightens” / “slightly lighter”).
  • Line-height tokens --text-*-leading, easing tokens --easing-*, layout tokens --header-height (56px), --sidebar-width*, --detail-panel-width, --drawer-width.
  • Empty-state copy and first steps for DASH-002 to DASH-011 (the spec writes only DASH-001’s); loading skeletons for every dashboard follow the DASH-001 pattern.
  • Collapsed sidebar shows one icon per section with a flyout (as the wireframe draws it).
  • Status colour is carried by icons and tinted backgrounds; text on them stays in the body colour, because amber and green on white fall under 4.5:1.

Contradictions kept and marked (REVISIT)

  • Touch targets: foundations 44/48px, ADR-011 44/56px, ux-decisions names --touch-target-min: 48px / --touch-target-field: 56px. The named tokens are used.
  • Expanded sidebar: 272px here vs 240px in foundations and the navigation-shell wireframe. At 240px, Spanish labels such as “Recaudación de fondos” do not fit on one line beside a scrollbar.
  • Collapsed sidebar: 64px (foundations, wireframe) vs 56px (navigation-shell Design Decisions). 64px used.
  • Partido Verde theme sets on-primary to white, but white on #16a34a is 3.3:1; the spec’s own contrast rule switches it to dark text, which is used.
  • DASH-003 lists a “Quarterly FEC filing” while DASH-007 calls the same Mar 15 deadline a CEE filing; both rendered as written.
  • DASH-005 “Needs Attention (6)” lists three items; rendered as written.
  • Detail panel shows “Support Score: 4 (Strong)” while field mode’s scale has 1 = Strong Support; rendered as written.
  • Field Ops chart is titled “30 days” but labelled W1–W8 in the shell wireframe; rendered as 30 daily points.
